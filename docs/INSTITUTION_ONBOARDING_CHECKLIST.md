## Pre-Onboarding Information Collection

### 1. Institutional Identity & Organization Details
- [ ] Institution Legal Name, Display Name, and Official Slug (`/onboarding`).
- [ ] Institution Type (K-12 School, Junior College, Degree College, Multi-Campus University).
- [ ] Official Email, Phone, and Physical Address (City, State, Postal Code, Country).
- [ ] High-Resolution Logo (`.png`/`.svg`) and Official Header for Receipts and Report Cards.
- [ ] Campus Definitions (Main Campus, Satellite Campuses).

### 2. Academic Hierarchy & Calendar Setup
- [ ] Active Academic Year (e.g., `2026–2027`) and Start / End Dates.
- [ ] Academic Terms (Term 1, Term 2, Semesters).
- [ ] Academic Departments (Science, Mathematics, Humanities, Commerce, Engineering).
- [ ] Programs and Degrees offered.
- [ ] Grade Levels / Classes (Grade 1 through Grade 12 / Year 1 through Year 4).
- [ ] Sections per Grade Level (`Section A`, `Section B`, etc.).
- [ ] Master Subject List with Subject Codes and Credit Hours.

### 3. Faculty & Staff Roster
- [ ] Staff List (Full Name, Official Email, Phone, Employee ID).
- [ ] Staff Role Allocations:
  - Administrator / Academic Administrator
  - Department Head
  - Faculty (Teaching Staff)
  - Staff (Office, Transport, Hostel, Library)
- [ ] Subject and Class Teacher Allocations.
- [ ] Staff Invitation Email Dispatch & Password Setup.

### 4. Student & Guardian Records
- [ ] Student Roster (Admission Number, Full Name, DOB, Gender, Grade, Section).
- [ ] Guardian Details (Father, Mother, Legal Guardian Full Name, Email, Phone).
- [ ] Student-to-Guardian Relationships mapped (`student_guardians`).
- [ ] Primary Contact designation for Automated Absence Notifications.
- [ ] Student Document Archive (Birth Certificates, Previous Transcripts, Medical Notes).

### 5. Daily Attendance Configuration
- [ ] Attendance Mode (Class-wise daily, Session-wise morning/afternoon, or Period-wise).
- [ ] Class Teacher Marking Authorization verified.
- [ ] Automated Absence Notification Channels configured (In-App, Push).
- [ ] Daily Attendance Cut-off Time established.

### 6. Timetable & Room Scheduling
- [ ] Room Master List (Room Numbers, Capacities, Lab/Lecture designations).
- [ ] Period Definitions (Period 1 to Period 8, Bell Timings, Break/Lunch slots).
- [ ] Weekly Slot Matrix generated and checked for faculty double-booking.
- [ ] Timetable published to student and parent portals.

### 7. Fee Structures & Accounting
- [ ] Fee Heads defined (Tuition, Laboratory, Library, Transport, Examination).
- [ ] Fee Structures created for each class/program.
- [ ] Payment Installment Schedules configured with Due Dates.
- [ ] Receipt Numbering Sequence Prefix set (e.g. `REC-2026-`).
- [ ] Late Fee Rules & Grace Periods established.

### 8. Transport & Fleet Setup (Where Applicable)
- [ ] Vehicle Master List (Registration Number, Capacity, Vehicle Type).
- [ ] Driver Profiles (Full Name, License Number, Contact Number).
- [ ] Routes and Pick-up / Drop-off Stops with planned timings.
- [ ] Student Transport Route Assignments.
- [ ] Driver Android Devices tested for GPS Journey broadcasting.

### 9. Pilot Sign-Off
- [ ] Administrative Administrator Sign-off.
- [ ] AUVRYN Technical Representative Sign-off.
