# AUVRYN — Teacher & Faculty User Guide

**Target Role:** Teaching Faculty, Class Teachers, Subject Teachers  
**Platform:** AUVRYN Web Application & Mobile App  

---

## 1. Teacher Dashboard & Schedule

When logging in as a faculty member, your home view shows:
- **Today's Teaching Schedule:** Periods, assigned classes, and room allocations.
- **My Assigned Classes:** Quick shortcuts to take daily attendance or enter exam marks.
- **Recent Announcements & Institutional Notices.**

---

## 2. Taking Daily Class Attendance

1. Navigate to **Attendance → Take Attendance** (`/dashboard/attendance/sessions`).
2. Select your designated **Class** and **Section** (e.g., `Grade 9 - Section A`).
3. Click **Start Session**. The student roster loads with default status set to **Present**.
4. Tap/click on students who are **Absent**, **Late**, or on **Approved Leave**.
5. Click **Submit Attendance Batch**.
6. **Automatic Notification:** For every student marked `Absent`, AUVRYN automatically queues a priority absence notification to their verified guardians.

---

## 3. Entering Examination Marks

1. Navigate to **Examinations → Enter Marks** (`/dashboard/examinations/results`).
2. Select the Exam Schedule (e.g., `Midterm Examination 2026`) and your Subject.
3. Enter marks obtained for each student.
4. The system automatically computes grade bands (e.g. `A+`, `B`) according to institutional grading scales.
5. Click **Save Marks**. Once verified by the department head, report cards become visible to parents.

---

## 4. Timetable & Classroom Allocations

1. Navigate to **Timetable → My Schedule** (`/dashboard/timetable/faculty`).
2. View your weekly periods across all classes and rooms.
3. If an unplanned absence occurs, submit a leave request under **Leave Management** (`/dashboard/leave`) to allow coordinators to assign substitutions.

---

## 5. Teacher AI Assistant

1. Open **AI Assistant** (`/dashboard/ai-assistant`) or access it via the Mobile App.
2. Generate lesson plan outlines, differentiated quiz questions, and study material suggestions grounded in your syllabus.
3. Create draft student feedback notes for review.
