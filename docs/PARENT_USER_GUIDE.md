# AUVRYN — Parent & Guardian User Guide

**Target Role:** Parents, Guardians, Family Sponsors  
**Platform:** AUVRYN Parent Portal (`/portal/parent`) & Mobile Android App  

---

## 1. Accessing the Parent Portal

1. Access the web portal at `https://<YOUR_SCHOOL_URL>/portal/parent` or launch the **AUVRYN Mobile App**.
2. Log in using the verified email registered during your child's admission.
3. If you have multiple children in the school, use the **Ward Switcher** at the top of the dashboard to toggle between them.

---

## 2. Real-Time Attendance & Absence Alerts

- **Attendance Overview:** View your ward's overall attendance percentage, days present, and historical calendar.
- **Immediate Push Notifications:** If your child is marked absent during morning roll call, you receive an automated push notification and portal message:
  > *"Attendance Alert — AUVRYN: Your ward was marked absent today. Please contact the institution if you need clarification."*
- **Notification Preferences:** You can customize your alert channels (Push, In-App) under **Notification Settings**.

---

## 3. Academic Timetable & Schedules

- View your child's weekly timetable, including subject timings, teacher names, and room numbers.
- Stay informed about test schedules and classroom activities.

---

## 4. Examination Results & Report Cards

- Access published examination marks, grades, and teacher remarks.
- Download verified PDF term report cards for official records.

---

## 5. Fees & Payment History

- **Pending Dues:** View current term fee assignments, installment breakdowns, and due dates.
- **Payment Receipts:** Access official receipts issued for counter cash, cheque, or electronic payments.
- **Zero Surprises:** Receive timely notifications before late fee grace periods expire.

---

## 6. Live School Bus Tracking (Where Enabled)

- When your child's bus driver starts their morning or afternoon journey, track the bus's live GPS location on the map in real time.
- View vehicle registration numbers, driver contact details, and boarding events.

---

## 7. Parent AI Companion

- Access the **Parent AI Companion** in the mobile app to receive guidance on:
  - Helping your child with difficult academic topics.
  - Understanding curriculum expectations and homework strategies.
  - Viewing constructive study recommendations based on published marks.
