# AUVRYN — Student User Guide

**Target Role:** Students (High School, Junior College, College / Degree Students)  
**Platform:** AUVRYN Student Portal (`/portal/student`) & Mobile Android App  

---

## 1. Student Dashboard

Log in using your student email or admission credentials to access:
- **Academic Progress:** Overall attendance percentage, active homework assignments, and upcoming examinations.
- **Weekly Schedule:** Today's class timetable with period times and teacher details.
- **Campus Announcements:** School events, holidays, sports days, and academic deadlines.

---

## 2. Daily Schedule & Classroom Finder

- View your full weekly period timetable.
- Room numbers are displayed for every period so you always know where your next lecture or laboratory session is held.

---

## 3. Library & Book Loans

- Search the school library catalog for required textbooks and reference books.
- Track currently borrowed books, loan due dates, and return statuses.
- Receive return reminders before library fines accrue.

---

## 4. Examination Results & Performance Tracking

- View published marks, subject percentages, and grade cards as soon as department faculty release them.
- Compare your performance across terms to identify subjects needing extra study focus.

---

## 5. Student AI Tutor

- Access the **Student AI Tutor** directly within the mobile app.
- **Concept Explanations:** Ask questions about complex math formulas, physics principles, literature concepts, or historical events.
- **Practice Quizzes:** Generate self-assessment quizzes to test your understanding before formal class examinations.
- **Guided Learning:** The AI Tutor does not just provide direct answers; it breaks down problem-solving steps to ensure deep conceptual mastery.
